Step 04 / Close & settle · 3 min read

Signing, registration and getting the keys

Prepare for signing, confirm that the deed has been registered and organise the first week in your new home.

Confirm what’s needed for signing

Ask the notary for a list of the final documents, amounts and payment dates. If you’ll be abroad or need help reading the deed, discuss representation and translation early enough to arrange them.

For a sale handled through the Greek digital property transfer file, the notary starts the file, requests authorisation from buyer and seller, prepares the deed and submits the signed deed to the land registry. Ask your notary whether your sale will follow this process and when you can expect confirmation of registration.

Keep your records together

Save the signed deed, proof of registration and receipts for payments and fees. Ask your accountant to check your E9 property declaration in myAADE. AADE can create an E9 automatically for eligible digital transfers. Confirm with your accountant that the record is correct and ask what you need to do, if anything, and by when. Check when to expect your annual ENFIA property tax assessment.

Use a handover checklist

Agree when you’ll receive the keys and who will meet you at the property. At the handover, walk through the items included in the sale and keep a copy of any agreed inventory.

  • Collect all keys, remotes and access codes. Check who can show you how to use the heating, hot water and alarm.
  • Take meter readings and ask the current owner for the details you need to arrange utility accounts.
  • Find the water shutoff and electrical panel. If the home will stand empty between visits, decide who can check on it and respond if something breaks.
