Step 01 / Shortlist & view · 3 min read

What to check at a viewing in Halkidiki

Compare homes on the details that matter once you move in, from beach access to parking and winter comfort.

Make a shortlist for the way you’ll live

Start with the three things you’ll use every week. If you’ll live there year-round, check the drive to shops and the condition of the heating. If it’s a holiday home, think about parking on arrival and who will look after it between visits.

Use the same checklist at each viewing. Ask for the floor plan and an inventory ahead of time if the home is offered furnished.

Walk the route and inspect the home

If the listing gives a distance to the sea, walk the route to the beach you would actually use. Take note of slopes, steps, crossings and how easy the route feels with bags or children.

  • Open windows and balcony doors. Check shutters and screens, and look for damp around frames, on lower walls and in rooms below ground.
  • Ask the agent to show you the parking space, each entrance and the areas that go with the home. Ask which outdoor areas are private and which are shared.
  • Ask to see the heating, air conditioning and hot water in use. If a home has several levels, check where you would put beds, desks and storage on each one.

Write down what still needs a document

Ask how the advertised floor area was measured and whether it includes balconies, an attic or storage. If a parking space, garden or separate entrance is part of your decision, ask how it appears in the property documents and flag it for your lawyer or engineer.

When you request a second viewing, send your availability and the two or three points you want to check. You’ll spend the visit getting answers you can use.
