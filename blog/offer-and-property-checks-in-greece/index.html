Step 03 / Offer & checks · 3 min read

Making an offer and checking the property

Know what to put in your offer, which records to ask for and what to clarify before paying a deposit.

Put the terms in writing

State the proposed price, what stays with the home, your target timeline and any conditions you need to agree. If you’re relying on financing, a satisfactory technical inspection or a particular parking space being included, raise it before you make a commitment.

If a deposit or reservation payment is requested, have your lawyer review the terms before you sign or pay. Confirm who will hold the money, how it is applied to the price and when it is refundable.

Ask your lawyer and engineer to check different things

Give your lawyer the seller’s title information and ask them to confirm ownership, any recorded charges or claims, and the rights that come with the home. Ask how parking, storage and outdoor areas are described in the deed or related records. Ask which records they can obtain for the property, including a cadastral sheet. The Gov.gr online copy service covers specific areas.

Ask an engineer to compare the property as you saw it with the approved plans and check any changes to the layout or use. If separate entrances matter because you want two units, ask whether that use or a formal division is feasible before you factor it into your offer.

Agree what happens if a check raises a problem

Keep a list of open questions and who can answer each one. If the parking arrangement or an alteration needs further paperwork, ask your advisers to explain the implications, the options and the effect on your timeline before you agree to proceed.
