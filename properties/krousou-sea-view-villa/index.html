About the property

The main residence in Krousou, on the eastern side of Kassandra, has 420 m² of space. It includes six bedrooms, four bathrooms, three living rooms and six terraces, with panoramic views of the sea.

A separate enclosed building of 120 m² has a kitchen, dining area and living room for entertaining.

The outdoor features include a swimming pool, a garden sauna and a wood-fired barbecue. The property has a water filtration system, two independent entrances and parking for at least eight cars.

The listing describes a main plot and a separate nearby parcel. Confirm the land measurements and title details before relying on either parcel.
