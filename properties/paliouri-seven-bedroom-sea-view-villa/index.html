About the property

This 530 m² villa in Paliouri stands on a 5,000 m² plot and is arranged over three levels. It has seven bedrooms, an open-plan kitchen and living room, four bathrooms and two separate WCs.

The property has sea views, balconies, a garden, a swimming pool, parking and storage. The specification includes individual oil heating, a fireplace, air conditioning, aluminium window frames, double glazing, insect screens, awnings, an alarm, a solar water heater and a barbecue.
