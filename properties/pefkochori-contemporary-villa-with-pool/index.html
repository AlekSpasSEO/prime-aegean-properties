About the property

This three-level villa between Pefkochori and Glarokavos was built in 2023 and is about 200 m from the sea. The ground floor has an open-plan living and dining area, a kitchen with an island and a guest WC, opening onto the pool terrace and pergola.

The first-floor description identifies two bedrooms and a bathroom. The lower level identifies two bedrooms, a bathroom, a playroom and storage. The listing separately states five bedrooms, so the full bedroom total needs confirmation.

The outdoor space is described as 550 m² for exclusive use, with a 3 × 7 m swimming pool, a 30 m² pergola, lawns, irrigation, terraces and a barbecue area. The specification lists heat-pump heating, air conditioning, double-glazed windows, a solar water heater and an alarm. The agent reports energy class A+; certificate confirmation is pending.
