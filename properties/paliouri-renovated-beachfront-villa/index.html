About the property

This three-level beachfront villa in Paliouri underwent a complete renovation in July 2024. The listing describes panoramic sea views and direct access to the beach.

The second level has two sea-view bedrooms, a bathroom and a veranda. The villa has a swimming pool, Italian floor tiles, marble staircases and multi-scene lighting.
