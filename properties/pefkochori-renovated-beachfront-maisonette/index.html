About the property

This beachfront maisonette in the centre of Pefkochori was fully renovated in 2026. The listing describes the land around the home as for its exclusive use.

The first level has two bedrooms, one of them the primary bedroom, each with its own bathroom. The second level has a living room, kitchen and bathroom. The third level has two bedrooms and a bathroom.

Outside there is a private pool, a courtyard, an outdoor shower and parking for two to three cars within the plot.
