About the property

This 133 m² first-floor apartment in Thessaloniki is about 150 m from the sea, near local amenities. The building has a lift, and the apartment was renovated in 2024.

The home comes fully furnished. It has three bedrooms with wardrobes, a living room, a kitchen with appliances, two bathrooms and a separate WC.

The apartment faces southwest and has a terrace, a storage room and a parking space under the building. Heating is individual natural gas, and there is air conditioning. The supplied energy class is A+.
