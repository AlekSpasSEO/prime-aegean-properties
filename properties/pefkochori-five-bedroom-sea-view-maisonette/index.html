About the property

This 175 m² maisonette is near the beach and village centre in Pefkochori, with a stated distance of 100 m to the sea. It is arranged over two levels.

The ground floor has a living room, kitchen, two bedrooms, a bathroom and access to a sea-view terrace. Upstairs are three bedrooms and a bathroom. One bedroom opens onto a 60 m² balcony with a sea view.

The source gives conflicting bathroom totals, so the total must be confirmed before a viewing. Outside, approximately 200 m² of land is described as for exclusive use, and a parking space is included.
