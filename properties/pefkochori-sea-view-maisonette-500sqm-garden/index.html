About the property

This 133 m² maisonette is arranged over two levels in a settlement of five homes in Pefkochori. It has an open-plan living room and kitchen, three bedrooms and two bathrooms. One bedroom and one bathroom are on the ground floor.

The property has a sea view, autonomous oil heating and a fireplace. Outside, the 500 m² garden is described as for exclusive use, with electric awnings and automatic irrigation. Two parking spaces and an outdoor storage room are included.
