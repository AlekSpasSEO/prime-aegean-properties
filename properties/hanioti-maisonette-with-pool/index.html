About the property

This 120 m² maisonette in Hanioti, in the Municipality of Pallini, is fully renovated and furnished. The sea and village centre are each about 150 m away, with shops, restaurants and bars within walking distance.

The semi-basement has a living room and a bathroom. The mezzanine has two bedrooms and two bathrooms, and the first floor has a further two bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Outside, a large terrace looks out to sea. The property has a private swimming pool and two parking spaces reserved for the home. It is described as ready for immediate occupancy.
